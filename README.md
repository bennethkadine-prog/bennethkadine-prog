## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🌿 Bellabeat Smart Device Usage Analysis

**Marketing Analytics | Python | Power BI**

Bellabeat wants to grow its membership offering. Using Fitbit data from
35 users as a proxy, I analysed how people actually wear, move with, and
sleep with a smart device to find where a membership could add value.

**Key findings**
- **Engagement is moderate, not high:** over 62 days, the most engaged
  user wore the device on 79% of days, and only 17 of 35 users wore it
  on more than 65% of days.
- **Everyday movement is the default, not workouts:** about 80% of
  recorded time is sedentary and 17% light movement, with only 3% moderate
  or hard exercise. Low-engagement users logged about half the light
  movement of everyone else, while their exercise minutes were similar.
- **Activity has a predictable rhythm:** weekday peaks at 12-2 pm and
  6-7 pm with a dip around 3 pm; weekends show one broad early-afternoon peak.
- **Sleep tracking is underused:** 11 of 35 users never logged sleep, and
  44% of logged nights were under 7 hours.

**Recommendation:** Position membership around everyday movement and habit
building rather than workouts, and time in-app nudges to the 3 pm dip.

**Tools:** `Python` `Pandas` `Power BI` `DAX` `Data Visualization`

## 🔎 My Approach to Data

**Ask → Prepare → Clean → Explore → Analyse → Visualise → Communicate**

I'm interested in more than producing numbers or dashboards. I want to understand what the data is saying, why the pattern matters, and what someone can actually do with the insight.
